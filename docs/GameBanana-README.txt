ProxyLaunch - mod launcher for Zenless Zone Zero
=================================================

REQUIREMENTS
  - Windows 10 or 11
  - XXMI Launcher with ZZMI installed
    https://github.com/SpectrumQT/XXMI-Launcher
    Open XXMI once and set up Zenless Zone Zero before using ProxyLaunch.

INSTALL
  1. Extract this zip anywhere (for example your Downloads folder).
  2. Double-click ProxyLaunch-Setup-x.x.x.exe and follow the installer.
  3. Open ProxyLaunch from the desktop or Start menu.

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not
  code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

UPDATES
  ProxyLaunch updates itself. When a new version comes out you get a popup
  inside the app: click "Update now", then "Restart & update".
  Your mods, loadouts, themes and settings are kept.

FIRST LAUNCH
  ProxyLaunch finds the game and XXMI automatically in most cases.
  If it can't, open Settings > Folders & game and pick them yourself.
  Press Play to start the game with your mods.
