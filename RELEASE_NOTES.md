What's new in 2.13.1

- Mod pictures in Browse now always fit the window. Tall or very wide pictures no longer make the page huge or get cut off, and every thumbnail can be scrolled to.
- Switching pictures is fixed: the viewer stays on the picture you picked when the full mod page loads. You can also use the arrow keys, and a counter shows which picture you're on.
- Stricter "SFW + NSFW toggles" filter: it now only shows real outfit mods that are safe by default or just a bit revealing. Nude edits and mods whose pictures are mostly nudity stay hidden, and only character skins can be NSFW toggle (UI mods with NSFW content stay 18+). Mods get checked again with the new rules.
