What's new in 2.14.0

- Much stricter content filter. Every mod is now checked before it can show in the SFW views, not only mods GameBanana labels NSFW. ProxyLaunch looks at all of a mod's pictures, its title and description, and the names of the files inside its download. Mods still being checked stay hidden until they pass.
- SFW only: just mods with no nudity and nothing skimpy or suggestive. If there's any doubt, a mod is kept out.
- SFW + toggles: adds Experimental mods. These are safe outfits that came with NSFW options. ProxyLaunch hides their NSFW pictures and turns off the NSFW options in the installed files, so they only work in their SFW state.
- NSFW (blurred): every mod, with NSFW pictures blurred until you click them.
- Show all: every mod, no filtering and no blur.
- Content labels are now set by ProxyLaunch only. The label picker in mod details has been removed.
