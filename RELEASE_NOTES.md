What's new in 2.14.14

- New "Get missing files" button for mods that are broken because files are missing. It downloads the mod from GameBanana again and copies in only the files that are missing. Your keys, settings and on/off state stay as they are.
- You'll find it on the mod's card, in its details, and at the top of the Library to fix every mod with missing files at once.
- If a file isn't in the GameBanana download either, ProxyLaunch tells you, since only the mod's author can fix that.
- A mod put back after a mix-up no longer keeps the other mod's cover picture.

Requires XXMI Launcher with ZZMI set up. Questions or bugs? Reach out on Discord: ghxty
