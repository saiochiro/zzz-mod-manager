What's new in 2.14.18

- Your mods now stay in step with the game. When ProxyLaunch starts, it makes the game's mod folder match your Library, so changes made while the app was closed are picked up. It checks again quietly every 15 minutes.
- Syncing is lighter: only files that actually changed are copied, and XXMI only re-reads your mods when something changed, so the game starts faster.
