What's new in 2.14.3

- SFW and NSFW labels now come straight from GameBanana again: a mod is NSFW when GameBanana gives it a content rating or NSFW visibility, or when its title or tags say so. The AI picture check is gone completely.
- Browse: SFW only shows every safe mod at once, with all of each mod's pictures, and opens with a full page instead of just a couple of mods.
- Library: Needs attention no longer gets stuck. Click the pill again, the "installed" pill, or Show all mods to see everything again, and picking a character always shows all of that character's mods.
- Pop-up messages and the activity panel are no longer see-through.
