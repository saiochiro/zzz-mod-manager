What's new in 2.14.15

Updates and loadouts
- Updating a mod now keeps it the same mod: your custom cover, your keys (including Tidy keys), its place in the outfit rotation and the loadouts it's in all stay.
- Mods with several downloads on GameBanana ask which one you want, and updates and repairs keep using that one.
- Stream Safe now only turns things off: mods marked NSFW, plus character mods that have no GameBanana label. Default puts back what it turned off.
- Main Team keeps one outfit on per character and leaves interface mods alone.

Backups
- Deleting a mod keeps a copy. Put it back from Recently deleted in the Library.
- "Go back to the previous version" in a mod's menu undoes an update.
- Settings has a Backups section that shows how much space backups use, and lets you put one back, delete it or clean up old ones.

Library
- Select several mods at once to turn them on or off, update them or delete them.
- Sort by name, date added, last updated or size.
- A card warns when two mods that are on change the same thing on a character, with a button to turn one off.
- Mods for characters ZZMI doesn't know yet are labeled that way instead of looking broken.
- Surprise me picks from all of a character's outfits, and All off can be undone.
- A mod's menu can no longer end up hidden behind the toolbar.

Other fixes
- ProxyLaunch checks there's enough free space before downloading or unpacking a mod, and says so plainly when there isn't.
- Without internet, your Library and switches work as usual, and Browse and update checks say you're offline instead of showing errors.
- Report a bug in Settings saves a zip you can send. Your Windows user name and home folder are removed from it.
