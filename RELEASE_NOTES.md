What's new in 2.10.2

- Cleaner character cursors. The ears, tails, wings and other extras are smaller now, so the pointer itself stands out.
- New Undying Flame cursor: glowing light-blade wings fanning out from the pointer, with a little heart.
- Real rain in Velvet Heist and Night Patrol: falling raindrops with splashes where they land. Velvet Heist also gets a flickering neon sign.
- Smoother backgrounds. Drifting clouds and mist now loop without jumping, and the roads in Ember Rush and Outlaw Dust have bikes coming and going.
